Privacy notice

How we use the information you send us through this website.

PRIVACY

Who we are

Cooling Tower Treatment, The Lightbox G16, 111 Power Road, Chiswick, London W4 5PY. Telephone 020 3633 6064.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, site postcode and the details you tell us about your site or system.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, arrange and deliver the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it's stored

Enquiries are passed securely through Cloudflare and stored in our customer records system, Airtable. These providers process data on our behalf under their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete enquiry details within 24 months. Customer records are kept for as long as we need them to meet our legal, insurance and compliance obligations.

Cookies and similar technologies

We only use analytics and advertising cookies if you say yes. When they're switched on, we use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics, which helps us see how many people visit and which pages are useful, and Google Ads, which lets us measure whether our adverts lead to enquiries and show our ads to people who've visited this site before. If you click one of our Google adverts, the ad click reference and campaign details from the web address are added to your enquiry so we can tell which adverts work.

If you say no, none of these are loaded and no analytics or advertising cookies are set. Your choice itself is saved in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again on every page. You can change your mind at any time using the link at the bottom of every page.

Google's own explanation of how it uses information from sites like ours is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you view a page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Call us on 020 3633 6064 or write to us at the address above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
