SVC 01
The programme

Your tower is warm, wet and full of air. Bacteria love that.

A cooling tower or evaporative condenser works by evaporating water. That's brilliant for rejecting heat, but it concentrates everything in the water, scrubs dust and organic matter out of the air, and keeps the whole lot at a temperature bacteria are very happy in. Left alone, you get scale on the heat exchangers, corrosion in the pipework, slime on the fill and, worst of all, conditions where Legionella can grow and be carried out in the drift.

A water treatment programme is how you keep a lid on all four. Under ACOP L8 and HSG274 Part 1, anyone in control of a wet cooling system needs one, and it needs to be monitored, recorded and reviewed. We set it up, supply and look after the dosing, visit on a fixed schedule and tell you straight away when something drifts out of range.

What we control

Four things a programme has to keep on top of

Get one wrong and the others usually follow. Scale shelters bacteria, corrosion products feed them, and fouling soaks up biocide before it can do its job.

01 / Scale

Hardness salts

As water evaporates, calcium and magnesium salts concentrate and plate out on hot surfaces. Inhibitor and sensible bleed rates keep them in solution.

02 / Corrosion

Metal loss

Steel, copper and galvanised surfaces all corrode at different rates. The inhibitor package is matched to what your system is made of.

03 / Fouling

Dirt and debris

Towers scrub dust, pollen and leaves out of the air. Dispersants, filtration where fitted, and regular cleaning stop it settling.

04 / Microbiology

Bacteria and biofilm

Biocides keep the general bacterial count down and stop biofilm forming, which is where Legionella likes to live.

05 / Records

Proof it's working

Every check and result goes into your log book, so you can show an inspector the programme is doing its job.

06 / Review

Adjusting as you go

Seasons, heat loads and water quality change. We review the programme and tweak it rather than leaving it on autopilot.

Questions

Water treatment programmes: common questions

Do we legally need a water treatment programme for our cooling tower?

If you have a wet cooling tower or evaporative condenser, ACOP L8 expects you to have a written scheme for controlling the Legionella risk, and in practice that means a water treatment programme with monitoring and records. HSG274 Part 1 sets out what that programme should cover.

Do you treat evaporative condensers as well as cooling towers?

Yes. Evaporative condensers (common on refrigeration and process cooling) carry the same kind of risk and need the same approach: treatment, monitoring, cleaning and records.

Can you take over from our existing contractor?

Yes. We'll review your current programme, dosing kit and records first, so nothing gets lost in the handover. If the current setup is fine, we'll say so rather than changing things for the sake of it.

Will you supply the chemicals?

Yes. We supply the treatment chemicals as part of the programme, along with safety data sheets and the information you need for your COSHH assessments.
